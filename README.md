Consolidated reference and interactive digital application for **GO No. 1438-HED** with **GO No. 1439-HED** Clarifications, Higher Education Department, Government of West Bengal.

Prepared by **Dr. Indrajit Chakraborty**, Malda College.

## ✨ Features

- **179 Comprehensive Q&As**: Thorough answers covering every clause, rule, and eligibility condition.
- **Stage Progression Coverage**: Full requirements for Assistant Professor & College Librarian (Level 10 → 11 → 12 → 13A → 14).
- **Official Order Texts**: Complete integrated text with exact clause-by-clause citations.
- **PBAS & Score Evaluator**: Self-assessment tables, API criteria, and grading guidance.
- **High-Resolution Page Scans**: Embedded high-resolution scan viewer for GO 1438-HED and GO 1439-HED.
- **WhatsApp & Social Media Sharing**: Full Open Graph meta cards for instant link previews on WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X.
